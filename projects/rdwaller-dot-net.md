I created this site in August of 2026. I wanted a published place where I could practice and
demonstrate skills related to development and universal design. It is my hope that this project will
remain a work in progress throughout my career.

I chose to keep the architecture rudimentary. It uses plain HTML/CSS/JS and Eleventy templating. The
plain languages give me a space to "get back to the basics" of web development and to maintain a
core, versatile skillset. The Eleventy templating allows me to drastically reduce repeated code
without compromising the use of the plain languages.

## About my use of AI for this site

While I understand that vibe-coding has an established role in development, I prefer to keep this
personal space of mine largely free of AI-generated code. The same goes for content. I do use AI to
handle repetitive and tedious tasks, and for research, suggestions, and rubber-ducking. To ignore
these capacities would be to ignore a wealth of opportunities for learning and idea-generation.
