[<cite>Don't Make Me Think</cite>](https://a.co/d/0fLtT1P3), by Steve Krug (2013)
: A witty foundational book on web usability explained using straightforward and often comedic
  language. Some of the screenshots are looking ancient by now but they are no less applicable
  to user experience.

[<cite>The Design of Everyday Things</cite>](https://a.co/d/0fFfqDIM), by Don Norman (2013)
: A classic of usability design. This book genuinely increased my design awareness. Since
  reading it I find myself considering the usability of myriad things from silverware to software.

[<cite>Design That Scales</cite>](https://a.co/d/07nC9n6X), by Dan Mall (2023)
: A comprehensive work on design systems. Explains their parts, strategies for convincing
  management of their worth, how to maintain them, and more.

[<cite>Universal Principles of Design</cite>](https://a.co/d/0dTVpE2K), by William Lidwell et al. (2023)
: An encyclopedia of 200 fundamental design principles with the principles sorted alphabetically.
  An excellent desk reference or coffee table companion.
